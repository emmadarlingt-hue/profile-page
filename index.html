How I see a brief

Envision

I design for the person on the other side of the screen — usually on a phone, usually in a hurry. Every choice on a page has to earn its place, and I'd rather ship something quiet and finished than something loud and half-built.

  • Restraint

    One accent colour, and it has exactly four jobs on this page. If a design needs more than that to work, the problem isn't the palette.

  • Evidence

    Nothing goes on a page that can't be checked. Real projects, real links, real dates; when two sources disagree, the published record wins.

  • Shipping

    Every project below is live at a real URL. A design that never reaches one is a sketch, and a sketch can't be judged.

Restraint is a design decision. The art is in the handfinish.
